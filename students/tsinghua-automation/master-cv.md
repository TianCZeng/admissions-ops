## Research

**Vision-Based Localization Methods for Mobile Robots**  
Undergraduate graduation project author, 2025-2026. Submitted 2026-05 and
defended 2026-06. Compared feature-based and direct visual odometry front ends
on an indoor mobile robot and reported trajectory drift, tracking losses, and
recovery behaviour across corridor, lobby, and laboratory sequences. Methods
include visual odometry, multi-view geometry, pose graph optimization, Python,
and C++.

**Loop-Closure Robustness for Visual SLAM in Repetitive Indoor Corridors**  
Research assistant, 2026-2027. Studies why appearance-based loop closure fails
in corridors whose visual structure repeats, and measures how often false
positives corrupt the pose graph relative to descriptor choice and geometric
verification thresholds. Methods include visual simultaneous localization and
mapping, place recognition, false-positive loop-closure analysis, PyTorch, and
ROS 2.

**Uncertainty Calibration for Monocular Depth Estimation**  
Course research project author, 2025. Measured how poorly a monocular depth
network's predicted confidence matched its actual error on indoor scenes, and
reported calibration curves before and after temperature scaling. Methods
include monocular depth estimation, uncertainty calibration, reliability
diagrams, and NumPy.
