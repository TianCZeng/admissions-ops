## Research

**Open-Vocabulary Object Detection for Indoor Service Robots**  
Undergraduate research assistant, 2025-2027. Evaluates how open-vocabulary
detectors transfer from web-scale image data to robot-collected indoor scenes,
with emphasis on failure cases under clutter and occlusion. Methods include
open-vocabulary object detection, vision-language models, robotics perception,
Python, and PyTorch.

**Domain Shift Analysis for Night-Time Driving Scene Segmentation**  
Summer research intern, 2026. Measured segmentation degradation between daytime
and night-time street scenes and summarized error patterns for small objects,
glare, and low-light boundaries. Methods include semantic segmentation, domain
shift evaluation, calibration analysis, and benchmark datasets.

**Capstone: Efficient Visual Retrieval for Museum Collections**  
Capstone project lead, 2026-2027. Builds a retrieval prototype for matching
exhibit photos to catalog metadata while tracking precision, recall, and
curator-reviewed failure modes. Methods include contrastive image-text
retrieval, approximate nearest neighbor search, evaluation metrics, and
technical writing.
